# Rooted: taking it to the next level

Ranked by impact for the effort. The first block is what makes Rooted a real
business; the rest makes it the best app in its category.

## 1. Launch foundations (do these first)

1. **Put it live on its own domain.** Front end on Vercel, API on Render or
   Railway, database on MongoDB Atlas. Everything is already set up for this.
2. **Privacy policy, terms and medical disclaimer** written by a lawyer.
   These are required for the App Store, HealthKit and payments.
3. **A clinical advisor:** a licensed clinician or registered dietitian who
   signs off on the content. This builds trust, and it is a marketing point.
4. **Privacy-safe analytics:** count which features people use, with no
   health data in analytics.
5. **Beta group:** 20 to 50 real users for 2 weeks, plus a short weekly survey.

## 2. Make it sticky (daily habit)

6. **Native iPhone app (Capacitor)** with push notifications. This replaces
   the calendar-file reminders. See `PHASE_NATIVE_APP.md`.
7. **Apple Health and Apple Watch:** a haptic breathing pacer on the wrist,
   sleep and HRV feeding the daily plan.
8. **Widgets:** a "1-minute calm" button, today's plan and streaks on the
   home screen.
9. **Two-way calendar sync** with Google and Microsoft sign-in, so work
   meetings update on their own instead of through a file import.
10. **Weekly recap:** "You breathed 42 minutes, walked 5 times, and slept
    better on days you did the wind-down." Show charts of check-in results
    before and after.

## 3. Personal and smart

11. **AI Coach** (`PHASE_AI_COACH.md`): a chat that builds the routine and
    puts it on the calendar. This is the natural paid tier.
12. **Adaptive plan:** the plan gets gentler after a bad night or a
    stressful week, and learns which practices people actually finish.
13. **Symptom and mood insights:** "Your mood is higher on days with morning
    light." Show correlation, never diagnosis.

## 4. Content that wins

14. **Rooted Pros** (`PHASE_PRO_PARTNERS.md`): verified yoga teachers,
    dietitians and breathwork coaches publish programs. Start with 5
    founding pros.
15. **Video and recorded voice** for Move and Breathe, with a real teacher's
    voice instead of the synthetic one.
16. **Recipes** tied to the Pantry and shopping list: tap "make this" and
    the ingredients go on the list.
17. **Seasonal and life-stage tracks:** pregnancy-safe, menopause, new
    parents, shift workers, students and seniors, each filtered by the
    safety flags.

## 5. Community and growth

18. **Circles:** small private groups (family, friends, a studio's clients)
    doing a program together, with gentle check-ins. There is no public feed
    and no likes, which keeps it healthy.
19. **Share cards:** a beautiful image of a finished program or a streak,
    for social media.
20. **Referral program:** give a month of Rooted Plus, get a month.
21. **Workplace wellness (B2B):** companies pay per employee. The
    work-calendar feature is perfect for this ("protect 10 minutes between
    meetings"). This is the biggest revenue lever.
22. **Healing Places map** (live) with partner retreats, hot springs and
    studios, which can earn booking commissions.

## 6. Money

23. **Rooted Plus subscription** through Stripe and App Store purchases. It
    includes the coach, all programs, Watch features and advanced insights.
    The core library stays free.
24. **Affiliate shopping list:** link pantry items to trusted retailers,
    always disclosed, with the same evidence bar.
25. **Pro subscriptions:** professionals host branded programs for their
    clients.

## 7. Trust and safety (keeps it the best)

26. **Accessibility audit:** screen readers, captions, larger text and
    reduced motion. Make it usable by everyone.
27. **Languages:** Spanish first.
28. **Crisis features reviewed** by a suicide-prevention organization, with
    988 and international lines kept current.
29. **Security review and backups** before real user data arrives.
