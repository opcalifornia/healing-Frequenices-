exists. Start from these pieces, then add the rest.

## To build when we pick this up

1. **Positioning and messaging:** one sentence, three proof points, voice guide.
2. **Launch plan:** pre-launch waitlist, beta stories, launch week, first 90 days.
3. **Content calendar:** the five pillars (proof, practice, myth vs fact, real
   people, giving back), a month of ready posts, short-video scripts built from
   the app's charts and breathing guides.
4. **Landing page:** the public website front door, with email sign-up.
5. **Email and notification sequences:** welcome series, first-week nudges,
   win-back messages, with the 60-second first win as the hook.
6. **Partnerships:** founding Pros, retreats and hotels, nonprofits (Earth &
   Give Back), local studios.
7. **Paid and PR:** small test budgets, press angle, podcasts, app-store page
   and screenshots.
8. **Measurement:** weekly numbers from the anonymous usage counts, and what
   to do when each one moves.
9. **Rules:** FTC disclosure, no health-outcome claims, sweepstakes law.
