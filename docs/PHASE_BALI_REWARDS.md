# Phase: Bali rewards (subscriber incentive)

**Status:** marked for later. Not built. Needs a lawyer before any public
announcement.

## The idea

Subscribers who stay for three months in a row earn a chance at a trip to
Bali, offered every quarter. The goal is to give people a reason to subscribe
and stay, and to give them something exciting to talk about.

## The one decision that changes everything: how is the trip given?

| Structure | How it works | Legal risk | Cost to us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Prize draw** | Everyone who has paid for 3 months in a row is entered; one winner per quarter | **High unless set up correctly.** Paying for a chance to win is the textbook definition of an illegal lottery in most states. A "no purchase necessary" free entry method is required, which weakens the incentive | One trip per quarter, a fixed cost no matter how many subscribers |
| **B. Earned reward** | Everyone who hits a milestone gets the same reward (for example 3 months = a badge, 12 months = a free month, 24 months = a retreat discount) | Low. It's a loyalty program, not chance | Scales with subscribers, so it must be small per person |
| **C. Community milestone** | "When Rooted reaches 5,000 members, we send one member to Bali, chosen by draw" | Medium. A draw is still involved | One trip per milestone, paid for after growth |

Our recommendation is **B for everyone, plus A as an occasional extra with a
free entry route**, once a sweepstakes lawyer has set it up.

## What it costs (estimates, to be replaced with real quotes)

**Trip for two for a week, from the US:** roughly $4,000 to $6,500. Flights
about $1,400 to $2,000 per person, a villa or hotel about $150 a night, food
and activities about $100 a day per person. A single-traveler trip is about
$3,000 to $3,500.

**What each subscriber brings in:** at $9.99 a month, about $8.49 a month
after Apple's 15% fee (the small-business program), $6.99 at the standard 30%
fee, or $9.67 through our own website payments. Three months is about $25.

**Break-even.** A $5,000 trip is paid for by about 196 people paying three
months, but only if every one of them signed up *because of* the trip. Many
would have subscribed anyway, so the real number is higher:

| Share of entrants who joined *because of* the promo | Entrants needed to cover a $5,000 trip |
|---|---|
| 100% | 196 |
| 50% | 393 |
| 30% | 654 |

**The trip as a share of quarterly subscription revenue ($5,400 including
admin and legal upkeep):**

| Paying subscribers | Revenue per quarter | Trip as % of revenue |
|---|---|---|
| 300 | $7,600 | 71% |
| 500 | $12,700 | 42% |
| 1,000 | $25,500 | 21% |
| 2,000 | $50,900 | 11% |
| 5,000 | $127,400 | 4% |
| 10,000 | $254,700 | 2% |

**Rule of thumb:** don't launch a full trip until you have about 2,000 paying
subscribers, so the trip is about a tenth of revenue. Before that, run a
smaller reward.

**The bigger payoff is retention.** If the promise raises the share of people
who stay past month three by 10 points, 1,000 new subscribers means 100 more
paying members, plus every month after that. A 10-point lift is a guess to
test, not a promise.

## Ways to make it cheaper (ideas to explore)

- **Partner with a Bali retreat or resort.** They often give rooms in exchange
  for exposure to our audience. This can cut the cost by half or more.
- **A single-traveler trip** is about $3,000 to $3,500.
- **Keep the prize at or under $5,000.** New York and Florida require
  registration and a bond for prize pools above $5,000 (confirm with counsel).
- **Start with a smaller first prize** (a local wellness weekend, a retreat
  voucher) and grow the prize with the member count, shown as a progress bar.

## Legal and platform rules to settle first

1. **Sweepstakes law.** A prize plus chance plus payment is a lottery. A
   sweepstakes needs a free "alternate method of entry" and official rules.
   Rules differ by state. Some states and countries ban certain promotions.
2. **Apple and Google rules.** Apple requires that the developer, not Apple,
   sponsors any contest, and that Apple be clearly not involved. Entry cannot
   be tied to an in-app purchase in some cases. Read the current App Review
   Guidelines before designing the entry flow.
3. **Taxes.** A prize is taxable income to the winner. Prizes over $600 need a
   tax form from us. We may choose to cover part of the winner's tax bill.
4. **Travel logistics and liability.** Passports, visas, travel insurance,
   cancellations, injuries. A written agreement and waiver for the winner.
5. **Advertising rules.** The FTC requires clear disclosure of terms and
   odds. Never imply the prize is guaranteed.
6. **Our own honesty.** Never promise health outcomes tied to the reward.

**Next step when we pick this up:** a one-hour consult with a sweepstakes and
promotions attorney (typically a few hundred to a couple thousand dollars
for rules and a compliance check), then build.

## What we'd build

- A "Your Rooted journey" tracker: months subscribed, streak, and progress
  toward each reward, with a share card ("3 months in!").
- Reward and entry screens with the official rules and a free-entry route.
- A winner-selection tool that is auditable and fair.
- Reward and entry data kept separate from health data.
